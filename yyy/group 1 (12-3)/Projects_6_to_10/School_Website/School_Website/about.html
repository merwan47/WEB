◆ Our Story

Who We Are

Yemane Birhan Secondary School has served the Addis Ababa community for over 18 years, providing quality education to thousands of Ethiopian students. We are a proud institution committed to nurturing the next generation of Ethiopian leaders.

Our school offers education from Grade 9 through Grade 12 in both Natural Science and Social Science streams. With over 1,200 enrolled students and more than 50+ dedicated teachers, we strive for academic excellence every single day.

We believe that every student has the potential to succeed, and we provide the environment, support, and encouragement to help them reach their goals.

◆ Our Community

Community & Environment

Our students are more than learners — they are active citizens. We participate in community service programs including Ethiopia's national tree planting campaigns, sports events, and cultural activities.

In 2017, our students proudly joined the 700 Million Seedlings national campaign, planting trees across Addis Ababa as part of the government's green initiative. We are committed to contributing to a greener, healthier Ethiopia.

Our students develop a strong sense of responsibility for their environment, their school, and their nation.
